# Keratoconus: condition and symptoms

Keratoconus changes the shape of the cornea, the clear front surface of the eye. The cornea becomes thinner and protrudes into a cone-like shape, affecting how it focuses light. Both eyes are usually affected, but not equally. Symptoms can include distorted or blurred vision, sensitivity to light and glare, and frequent prescription changes. Rapid worsening of eyesight warrants assessment by an eye doctor. Symptoms alone do not establish a diagnosis.
