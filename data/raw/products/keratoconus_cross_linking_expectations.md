# Keratoconus: cross-linking expectations

Cross-linking for keratoconus aims to prevent further deterioration. Patients may still need glasses or contact lenses afterward. Temporary discomfort and hazy vision can occur, and the procedure carries risks, including possible reduced vision. Suitability, technique, recovery, and expected benefit require an individual clinical discussion.
