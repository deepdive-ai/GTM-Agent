# Keratoconus: causes and progression

The exact cause of keratoconus is not fully understood; genetic and environmental influences are implicated. The condition often develops in adolescence or early adulthood. Its severity and speed of progression differ between people. Family history and allergic conditions can be relevant to the clinical history.
