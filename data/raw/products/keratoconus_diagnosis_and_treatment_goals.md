# Keratoconus: diagnosis and treatment goals

Assessment of keratoconus can include medical and family history, a vision/refraction examination, slit-lamp examination, and corneal imaging. Topography maps corneal shape; tomography can also assess thickness. Treatment has two distinct goals: improve vision and limit progression. The approach depends on severity and whether the condition is changing. Cross-linking may be recommended for progressive disease to help stabilize the cornea. It is not a promise of perfect vision.
