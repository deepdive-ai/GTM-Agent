# Scleral lens surface wear

Status: Assistant-prepared paraphrase of hospital patient guidance; clinical review pending.
Prepared and source accessed: 3 October 2026.

Moorfields Eye Hospital explains that the surfaces of gas-permeable scleral lenses can deteriorate through repeated wearing and handling. Re-polishing a lens is not always an option. Careful handling helps preserve the lens surface.

Source: Moorfields Eye Hospital, Care of your scleral contact lenses, section Taking care of your contact lenses.
https://www.moorfields.nhs.uk/for-patients/visual-aids/contact-lenses/care-of-your-scleral-contact-lenses

Scope: Explains surface wear. This source does not establish an individual replacement date or a clinic's replacement policy.
