# Keratoconus campaign editorial guidance

Writing rules, not medical evidence.

- Never use em dashes (U+2014) in generated content. Use commas, colons, parentheses, or separate sentences instead.
- Keep factual claims traceable to their source URLs.
- Do not promise cures, perfect vision, guaranteed outcomes, or freedom from glasses.
- Do not diagnose readers or infer their treatment eligibility.
- Do not present typical ages, success rates, recovery times, or follow-up intervals as universal.
- Do not imply avoiding eye rubbing guarantees prevention or reverses disease.
- Verify local approvals, availability, costs, and clinic services separately.
- Obtain clinical review before publishing medical content.
