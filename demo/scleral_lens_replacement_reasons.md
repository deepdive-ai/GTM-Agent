# Reasons a scleral lens may need replacement

Status: Assistant-prepared paraphrase of a professional clinical article; clinical review pending. This is professional guidance, not a randomized study.
Prepared and source accessed: 3 October 2026.

The clinical article identifies deposit accumulation, damage such as surface cracking or breakage, and altered lens fit or prescription power as reasons a scleral lens may need replacing.

A replacement is not necessarily an identical copy. Depending on the case, the existing design may be duplicated, adjusted, or replaced through a new fitting. Changes in an ectatic cornea can alter the clearance beneath a scleral lens.

Source: Dr. DeNaeyer, Replacing and Refitting Scleral Contact Lenses, Contact Lens Spectrum, 1 November 2018.
