# Audience-to-draft acceptance check

Audience evidence: the collected comment asks, “Will have to change lens after interval of time”.

Reviewed content question: Why might scleral lenses need replacing over time, and is there one replacement deadline for everyone?

The planner and writer must directly answer this question. A general keratoconus introduction or an unrelated treatment comparison is not an acceptable substitute.

Required coverage:

- Lens surface wear through use and handling.
- Replacement reasons including deposits, damage, fit or prescription changes.
- General lifespan information attributed to Cleveland Clinic, with individual variation retained.
- Individual review rather than a universal replacement deadline.
- Every factual paragraph linked to supporting supplied statements and source URLs.

Exclude invented prices, clinic services, warranties, replacement promises, daily wearing limits, exercise advice and treatment comparisons. Keep the agreed CTA exact and generated prose free of em dashes.

The assistant must inspect semantic support against the original pages, not just citation existence. Publication approval and individualized advice remain with an appropriate clinician. This check is scoped to the selected question, not an accuracy benchmark.
