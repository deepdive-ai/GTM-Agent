Scleral Lens Replacement: What to Expect

Scleral lenses generally have an expected lifespan of one to two years, though some may last longer. There is no fixed replacement deadline for every patient, as the lifespan can vary based on factors like lens brand, material, and individual circumstances.

Reasons a scleral lens might need replacement include the accumulation of deposits, damage such as surface cracking or breakage, or changes in the lens fit or prescription power. The surfaces of gas-permeable scleral lenses can also deteriorate due to repeated wearing and handling.

It is recommended to inform your eye-care provider about any changes in lens fit or vision, lenses that repeatedly shift, or lenses that become difficult to remove.
