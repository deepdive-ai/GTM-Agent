# Keratoconus: vision correction and monitoring

Glasses or soft contact lenses may help in earlier keratoconus. Specialist contact lenses may be needed as corneal shape becomes more irregular. Advanced cases with inadequate vision despite lenses may require consideration of transplantation. Follow-up examinations and scans help clinicians assess changes and decide whether additional treatment is appropriate.
