# Scleral lens lifespan and follow-up

Status: Assistant-prepared paraphrase of medically reviewed hospital patient information; clinical review of this summary pending.
Prepared and source accessed: 3 October 2026.

Cleveland Clinic gives one to two years as a general expected lifespan for scleral lenses, while noting that some last longer. An individual's expected lifespan depends on factors such as lens brand, material and personal circumstances. This is general information, not a fixed replacement deadline for every patient.

Cleveland Clinic advises telling an eye-care provider about changes in lens fit or vision, lenses that repeatedly shift out of place, or lenses that become difficult to remove. Follow-up visits and their schedule are recommended by the eye-care provider.
