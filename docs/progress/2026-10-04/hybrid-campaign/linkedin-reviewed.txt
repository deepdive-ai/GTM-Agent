Understanding the Lifespan of Scleral Lenses for Keratoconus

According to Cleveland Clinic, scleral lenses generally have an expected lifespan of one to two years, though some may last longer. This general information is not a fixed replacement deadline for every patient, as the actual lifespan can vary based on factors such as the lens brand, material, and personal circumstances.

The surfaces of gas-permeable scleral lenses can deteriorate over time due to repeated wearing and handling. Careful handling of scleral lenses can help preserve their surface.

A scleral lens might need replacement for reasons such as the accumulation of deposits, damage like surface cracking or breakage, or changes in lens fit or prescription power. It is advisable to inform an eye-care provider if there are changes in lens fit or vision, if lenses repeatedly shift out of place, or if they become difficult to remove.
